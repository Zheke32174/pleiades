# Defensive Use Disclaimer

Pleiades is an owner-authorized defensive research project.

## Intended Uses

- Local lab environments on hardware you own or administer
- Defensive telemetry collection with owner awareness
- Decoy service testing and honeypot research
- Forensic evidence handling and incident response support
- Container recovery and rebuild testing
- Policy-gated automation research

## Not Intended For

- Unauthorized deployment on third-party systems
- Stealth installation without owner knowledge
- Credential theft or unauthorized access
- Lateral movement between systems
- Anti-forensic operations against a legitimate owner
- Evasion of authorized monitoring or administration
- Any use that violates applicable laws or policies

## Operator Responsibility

The operator is solely responsible for:
- Ensuring authorization before deploying on any system
- Complying with applicable local, national, and international laws
- Complying with applicable organizational policies
- Responsible handling of any forensic evidence collected
- Keeping credentials, evidence archives, and private metadata private

The maintainers of Pleiades accept no responsibility for misuse of this software.
