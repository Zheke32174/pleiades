# Full Ecology Integration Readiness

This tranche implements repository-achievable progression points 91 and 93–98
from `modos/ECOLOGY_PROGRESSION_100.md` and preserves explicit blockers for
points 92, 99, and 100.

`ecology_integration.py` evaluates whether the public contracts form a coherent
pre-live operating ecology. It does not claim that the live system exists.

## Ecology and ontology binding

The candidate binds public and private ecology digests to executive authority,
workspace, learning-spine, and distributed-admission receipts. Required typed
relations connect those surfaces.

A full ontology closure must declare both public and private scopes, refuse
seed-only status, and exceed bounded object and relation floors. However, point
92 remains blocked until a receipt is generated against the actual live private
registry rather than a synthetic fixture.

## Service learning joints

Every admitted service joint must emit structured:

- observations;
- decisions;
- evidence;
- outcomes;
- corrections;
- provenance.

Blind self-training is forbidden. Joint provenance is digest-bound.

## Predictive simulation and calibration

A candidate must carry a replayable, non-mutating simulation over at least ten
scenarios and bind counterfactual evidence. Predicted success is compared with
observed outcome evidence. Calibration error must remain within promoted policy.

This supplies the deterministic seam for the predictive virtualisation and
hyperbolic-chamber workflow without pretending simulation is deployment.

## Three-part improvement criterion

Promotion readiness requires:

1. machine-measurable improvement;
2. independent steward verification;
3. ordinary-person-noticeable improvement for user-facing changes.

User-facing evaluation must be blinded, contain at least five trials, and clear
a minimum preference threshold.

## External treaty boundary

Cross-Mind and external-agent treaties must be:

- scoped rather than wildcarded;
- reciprocal;
- revocable;
- replay-resistant;
- unable to delegate constitutional authority.

The readiness gate does not activate a treaty.

## Honest terminal status

The receipt status is `repository-ready-live-blocked`. It retains blockers for:

- a live-verified private-registry ontology compile;
- the actual admission → observation → rollback → learning loop;
- sustained bounded autonomous operation.

These cannot be proven by repository fixtures.
