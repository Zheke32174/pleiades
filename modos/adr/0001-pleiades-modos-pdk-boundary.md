# ADR-0001: Pleiades, MODOS, and PDK boundaries

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-07-17

## Decision

Pleiades is the greater governed computing world and constitutional architecture. MODOS is the concrete Modular Ontological Distributed Operating System implementation within Pleiades. The PDK is MODOS's deterministic over-kernel.

Existing repositories retain their identities. They become organs behind stable contracts rather than being renamed or collapsed into a monorepo.

## Consequences

- `pleiades` remains a defensive substrate and becomes the public MODOS/PDK integration surface.
- `undergrowth` is the private resource spine and exhaustive repository ontology.
- `understory` remains the running agent/harness organism.
- RYZ and Ryznix are node substrates and future native implementations, not the PDK itself.
- Backups and recall snapshots are evidence-bearing lineage objects, not active canon.
- Models and agents cannot manufacture capabilities or mutate kernel policy directly.
