## Provenance rules

1. Unified Canon v2 governs conflicts.
2. Later explicit steward ADRs may refine canon but must remain reviewable.
3. Scope-specific blueprints refine their domain without silently overriding unrelated authority or promotion law.
4. Historical papers, branches, transcripts, Drive junkyard material, and backups supply evidence; they do not silently override current canon.
5. Generated Rust, protobuf, schemas, fixtures, and configuration remain traceable to a source obligation or documented synthesis decision.
6. Transformation does not increase authority or erase provenance, identity, negation, taint, visibility, dissent, uncertainty, or failure evidence.
7. Public artifacts do not disclose private repository paths, raw evidence, secrets, or private RYZ implementation details.
8. Schema validation, compilation, simulation, and live-system validation are separate receipts.
9. A valid signature, green test, or successful simulation does not self-authorize promotion or deployment.
